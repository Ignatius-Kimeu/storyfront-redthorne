The story behind the stitch.

Three ideas stitched into one name — and a shop built around the fabric to back it up.

What's in a name

RED for power and presence. THORN for the tailor's needle. HORN, a quiet nod to Ankole cattle and the heritage of the founder's family — three ideas, stitched into one word.

The Fabric

Every piece begins with the bunch books — binders of imported fabric samples from established European and Italian mills, the same houses that supply the world's better-known tailoring names.

It's a slower way to build a wardrobe: choose the cloth, take the measurements, wait for the fit rather than settle for it.
